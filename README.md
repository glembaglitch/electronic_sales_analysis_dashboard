# Electronics Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI report summarizes electronics sales performance across revenue, profit, products, payment methods, cities, sales representatives, and order dates. The dashboard is designed to help users spot leading products and categories, compare sales channels and locations, and follow revenue trends over time.

## Dashboard contents

The `DASHBOARD` page includes:

### Key metrics

- **Total revenue** — overall sales amount.
- **Average discount** — average discount applied to sales.
- **Average profit** — average profit value.
- **Total profit** — overall profit.

### Visuals

- **Sales by product category** — compares sales across Computers, Home Appliances, and Electronics.
- **Sales by payment method** — compares sales for Credit Card, PayPal, and Debit Card.
- **Revenue and profit by product category** — shows revenue and profit together by category.
- **Revenue share by payment method** — shows each payment method's portion of revenue.
- **Sales amount by city** — ranks cities by sales amount.
- **Profit by category** — compares profit across product categories.
- **Revenue by product** — ranks products by revenue.
- **Revenue by sales representative** — compares revenue across representatives.
- **Profit by product category** — compares category profit in a separate ranked view.
- **Average profit by discount** — shows average profit at discount levels.
- **Revenue trend by order date** — displays how revenue changes over time.

## How to use the dashboard

- Select a bar, point, or segment in a chart to cross-filter or highlight related values in other visuals.
- Select the same item again or click a blank area of the canvas to clear the selection.
- Hover over a data point to see its detailed value in a tooltip.
- Compare the KPI cards with the charts to move from the overall result to a product, location, payment method, or time period.

## Data fields

The report's visible model includes fields for sales amount, profit, discount, quantity, product category and name, payment method, order date, delivery date, city, country, region, customer, and sales representative. The dashboard visuals aggregate these fields to present the comparisons above.

## Notes

Displayed totals depend on the report's current filters and selections. Confirm the selected period and any active visual selections before comparing figures or sharing results.
